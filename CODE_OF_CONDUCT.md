# Code of Conduct

Termix is built by people from many places, and we want everyone to feel welcome here: in issues, pull requests, Discord, and anywhere else you act for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Respect that people disagree, and that maintainers make the final call.
- Accept responsibility when you get something wrong, and fix it.

## What is not okay

- Insults, slurs, or attacks on someone's identity or background.
- Harassment, in public or private, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or derailing discussions on purpose.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as unwelcome in a professional space.

## Reporting

If something happens to you or you see it happen, report it privately:

- On GitHub, use Report content on the comment, issue or pull request (in its "..." menu). Choose to report it to the repository maintainers. Only maintainers see these reports.
- Or send a direct message to a maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) on the [Termix Discord](https://discord.gg/jVQGdvHDrf).

Reports are kept private. We will reply within a week. A maintainer involved in a report is left out of handling it.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what to do based on what happened, from a private warning, to removing content, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. We tell the person reported what they did and what the outcome is, and we never punish anyone for a report made in good faith.
